How it works

No feeds to scroll, no profiles to swipe. Just things that are actually happening near you.

1

See what's nearby

Browse a map or a list of Assemblies within a few miles, by time and by kind.

2

Join in one tap

Join right away, or ask the host if they like to approve people first. Chat with the group before you go.

3

Show up

Meet at the place and time. Or host your own: pick a spot, a time and how many seats.

Built to keep you safe

Meeting new people should feel comfortable. Assembly is designed around that.

No live location sharing

Nobody can see where you are. Your location is only used on your phone to show what's near you.

Hosts choose what to share

Show the exact venue to everyone, or only an approximate area until someone joins.

Block and report

Block anyone, and it works both ways. Report anything that doesn't feel right.

Sign in with Apple

Sign in without handing over your email. You choose the name others see.
